<p><i>JellyBook</i> is a book and comic reading app for Jellyfin. Its a app thats meant to allow you to read your files from Jellyfin on mobile. Supported file formats include:</p><ul><li><code>.cbz</code> &amp; <code>.zip</code>: another Comic book format</li><li><code>.epub</code>: Electronic PUBlication is a wide-spread eBook format</li></ul><p>PDF and CBR/RAR archives are intentionally not supported by JellyBook.</p>
